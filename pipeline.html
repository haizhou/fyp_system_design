Four-stage System Pipeline

A confidence marker is created early, updated during reasoning, enforced during verification, and surfaced in the final answer.

Step 1
Query Understanding
Natural language is translated into a schema-valid KG query with entities, relations, constraints, answer type, reasoning goal, and confidence markers.
Step 2
Multi-hop Reasoning
SaulLM selects a strategy; R-GCN guided beam search expands paths, recovers from dead ends, and propagates confidence.
Step 3
Candidate Verification
Answer type and all constraints are checked as hard filters, separate from the soft scores used during beam search.
Step 4
Answer Generation
Verified KG paths are verbalised and passed to SaulLM to produce a natural answer with explicit confidence labels.
